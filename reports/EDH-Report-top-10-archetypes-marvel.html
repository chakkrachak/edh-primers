🎴 Top 10 Commander Archetypes — Marvel Super Heroes · EDH Report

This report breaks down the 10 best Commander archetypes from the Yoonns MTG ranking, in English. For each archetype you get the deck strategy (principle, core strengths, key weakness) plus the most popular MARVEL SUPER HEROES generals by EDHREC deck count — but only those that have a direct EDHREC page for that archetype (48 generals across the 10 archetypes). Sources: EDHREC (deck counts), Scryfall (Oracle text & card art), and the Yoonns MTG video "Les 10 meilleurs types de decks COMMANDER !" (translated into English).

GeneralGame Plans (top 5 EDHREC)
🪙 Tokens — Video #1
  • Strategy core: Multiply small creatures across the board, then amplify their stats in one explosive turn or convert the swarm into direct damage.
  • Main strengths: Ease and redundancy — a wall of blockers, draw engines like Skullclamp, and several game-enders (Craterhoof-style pumps, mass evasion, mass sacrifice) make the deck hard to stop.
  • Key weakness: A single board wipe resets the field — but the deck rebuilds fast because the command zone keeps feeding the engine.
  • Top general: The Unbeatable Squirrel Girl is the archetype's biggest Marvel draw, cranking out an exponential wall of Squirrel tokens that turns the token plan into a lethal swarm.
The Unbeatable Squirrel Girl7,028 decks
The most popular Marvel token commander, flooding the board with an ever-growing army of Squirrels.
General adds: The Unbeatable Squirrel Girl creates a Squirrel token on entry and on attack, then explodes into the swarm with her {1}{G}{G}{G} ability that spawns a token for each Squirrel you control.
Ultron, Artificial Malevolence4,303 decks
Clones every nontoken artifact you play into an army of 2/2 Robot tokens.
General adds: Ultron, Artificial Malevolence pays {2} when another nontoken artifact enters to copy it, and non-creature copies come in as 2/2 Robot Villain artifact creatures.
Ultron, Unlimited1,349 decks
A connive-fueled Robot-token engine built on card advantage.
General adds: Ultron, Unlimited connives on attack and creates a 2/2 Robot Villain token whenever a creature you control connives.
Silk, Web Weaver1,229 decks
A tokens-and-humans build that also pumps the whole attacking team.
General adds: Silk, Web Weaver makes a Human Citizen token on every creature spell you cast and gives all your creatures +2/+2 and vigilance.
Spider-Ham, Peter Porker438 decks
A zoo-style token deck with animal-tribe synergy and Food to keep going.
General adds: Spider-Ham, Peter Porker creates a Food token on entry and pumps an army of animal-tribe creatures by +1/+1.
☠️ Poison — Video #2
  • Strategy core: Win by landing 10 poison counters instead of draining 40 life, effectively quartering the victory threshold.
  • Main strengths: With proliferate, a single counter can finish off every opponent from afar without needing to attack again.
  • Key weakness: Immediate political liability — the whole table gangs up on you as soon as the first counters start landing.
  • Top general: The Serpent Society is the truest Marvel infect commander, landing poison counters through deathtouch and forcing opponents to sacrifice creatures. Marvel fields few dedicated infect commanders — only 3 (The Serpent Society, Spider-Man India, Morlun) have a direct EDHREC infect page, so the archetype is shown with those 3.
The Serpent Society3,880 decks
The flagship Marvel infect commander, pairing poison counters with deathtouch and sacrifice value.
General adds: The Serpent Society has deathtouch and a ward paid in poison counters, and whenever another deathtouch creature you control dies, each opponent sacrifices a nontoken creature.
Spider-Man India259 decks
A web-slinging hero that grows creatures and clears the skies around your poison threats.
General adds: Spider-Man India puts a +1/+1 counter on a creature and gives it flying each time you cast a creature spell.
Morlun, Devourer of Spiders212 decks
A big-mana X-cost general that pressures life totals the poison way.
General adds: Morlun, Devourer of Spiders enters with lifelink and X +1/+1 counters, dealing X damage to an opponent on entry.
🧟 Reanimator — Video #3
  • Strategy core: Dump big creatures into the graveyard, then bring them back for a fraction of their mana cost.
  • Main strengths: Blistering speed — an Archon of Cruelty can hit the board by turn four — plus easy reanimation loops and infinite combos.
  • Key weakness: Sensitive to graveyard hate, which casual pods don't always run in quantity; one well-timed exile can stall the plan.
  • Top general: Anti-Venom, Horrifying Healer is the archetype's top Marvel draw, returning a creature from your graveyard to the battlefield the moment he arrives.
Anti-Venom, Horrifying Healer11,682 decks
The most-built Marvel reanimator, returning a key creature for free on entry and becoming a regenerating wall.
General adds: Anti-Venom, Horrifying Healer returns a creature card from your graveyard to the battlefield on entry, and converts damage into +1/+1 counters on himself.
Taskmaster, Mercenary Mimic1,144 decks
A shapeshifting reanimator that copies any creature or graveyard card each turn.
General adds: Taskmaster, Mercenary Mimic becomes a copy of a creature on the battlefield or a creature card in a graveyard, letting you clone your best reanimation targets.

General adds: Ultron the Annihilator creates a 2/2 Robot token on entry and attack, and makes each opponent lose 1 life whenever an artifact is put into your graveyard.
Carnage, Crimson Chaos891 decks

General adds: Carnage, Crimson Chaos returns a creature card with mana value 3 or less from your graveyard to the battlefield, eager to swing.
Symbiote Spider-Man852 decks

⛰️ Ramp/Landfall — Video #4
  • Strategy core: Drastically accelerate mana production to drop expensive payoff spells far ahead of curve — a 7-drop threat as early as turn four.
  • Main strengths: Complete inevitability: lands don't disappear, so even if an Eldrazi dies, a bigger threat is coming soon.
  • Key weakness: Can stall before the curve if the initial ramp spells don't show up in the opening hand.
  • Top general: Mole Man, Moloid Master supercharges the plan by letting you play lands from your graveyard and spawning Moloid tokens on every landfall.
Mole Man, Moloid Master592 decks
A lands-matter commander that recurs lands from the graveyard and builds an army from landfall.
General adds: Mole Man, Moloid Master lets you play lands from your graveyard and creates a 1/1 Moloid token each time a land enters.
Sandman, Shifting Scoundrel542 decks
A landfall voltron that grows as your lands grow and recurs lands from the graveyard.
General adds: Sandman, Shifting Scoundrel's power and toughness match your land count and he evades small blockers, returning himself plus a land from the graveyard.
Silver Surfer, Cosmic Voyager107 decks
A blink-style ramp general that exiles and replays your board, triggering landfall repeatedly.
General adds: Silver Surfer, Cosmic Voyager exiles any number of your permanents on entry and returns them at the next end step, replaying lands for extra triggers.
Spider-Man, Brooklyn Visionary90 decks
A reliable ramp piece that fetches a basic land while keeping your creature count high.
General adds: Spider-Man, Brooklyn Visionary searches your library for a basic land and puts it onto the battlefield tapped on entry.
Ka-Zar of the Savage Land86 decks
A top-of-library ramp commander that plays lands off the top and grows a companion.
General adds: Ka-Zar of the Savage Land lets you play lands from the top of your library and creates Zabu, a landfall-growing Cat token.
🌿 Enchantress — Video #5
  • Strategy core: Build the deck around a large number of enchantments, typically in Selesnya (Green-White).
  • Main strengths: A nearly inexhaustible draw engine, excellent ramp through land auras, and lockdown effects like Ghostly Prison.
  • Key weakness: Win conditions are often weaker than the engine itself, and big enchanted creatures are vulnerable to targeted removal.
  • Top general: SP//dr, Piloted by Peni is the closest Marvel analog to an enchantress engine, drawing a card every time a modified creature connects.

💚 Lifegain — Video #6
  • Strategy core: Raise your life total constantly to trigger abilities and treat life as a spendable resource.
  • Main strengths: Shrinks the risk of dying to attacks, and the cushion shines in Archenemy (1 vs 3) games where you're outnumbered.
  • Key weakness: Inefficient at high power against infinite combos or alternative damage that ignores your life total.
  • Top general: Mister Negative is the archetype's top Marvel general, weaponizing life gain and life exchange to race ahead of the table.
Mister Negative5,762 decks
A life-exchange engine that turns lifegain and inversion into raw card draw.
General adds: Mister Negative has vigilance and lifelink and, on entry, can exchange life totals with an opponent — drawing a card for every life gained.
Gwenom, Remorseless4,568 decks
A competitive lifegain/life-pay commander built around paying life to play from the top.
General adds: Gwenom, Remorseless has deathtouch and lifelink, and on attack lets you play cards from the top of your library by paying life equal to their mana value.
Aunt May439 decks
A pillow-fort lifegain that rewards every creature you cast with life.

General adds: Morlun, Devourer of Spiders enters with X counters and lifelink, dealing X damage to an opponent on entry to pad your cushion further.
Spider-UK56 decks
A life-payoff that draws and gains life whenever your creatures multiply.

🌀 Blink — Video #7
  • Strategy core: Temporarily exile your creatures so they return and re-trigger their enter-the-battlefield (ETB) effects.
  • Main strengths: A phenomenal card-advantage and control engine, and one of the hardest decks to interact with — blinking in response thwarts removal.
  • Key weakness: Often lacks fast, direct win conditions, especially in Azorius (White-Blue).
  • Top general: The Mighty Thor, Jane Foster is the archetype's top Marvel general, exiling a permanent on every attack and re-triggering its enter-the-battlefield effect.
The Mighty Thor, Jane Foster1,283 decks
A blink voltron that re-fires an artifact or creature's enter-the-battlefield effect on every attack.
General adds: The Mighty Thor, Jane Foster exiles up to one nontoken artifact or creature on attack, returns it tapped, and draws a card each time an Equipment enters.
Silver Surfer, Cosmic Voyager107 decks

General adds: Silver Surfer, Cosmic Voyager exiles any number of your permanents on entry and returns them at the next end step, re-triggering ETBs.

General adds: Spider-UK draws a card and gains 2 life whenever two or more creatures entered under your control that turn.

👑 Superfriends — Video #8
  • Strategy core: Build the whole plan around deploying and protecting a large number of Planeswalkers.
  • Main strengths: Overwhelming value every turn, with a nasty synergy on board wipes — Planeswalkers survive while creatures die.
  • Key weakness: Master pieces are expensive on mana, so deployment is slow and vulnerable early in the game.
  • Top general: Captain America, Team Leader leads Marvel's superfriends-context builds, rewarding every Hero that enters with haste, vigilance, and a pile of +1/+1 counters.
Captain America, Team Leader4,479 decks
A Hero-tribal commander that spreads vigilance and haste, turning every new Hero into an immediate attacker.
General adds: Captain America, Team Leader grants each entering Hero vigilance and haste and puts a +1/+1 counter on that Hero and on himself.

💀 Aristocrats — Video #9
  • Strategy core: Continuously sacrifice your own creatures to trigger recurring effects — life loss, mana, and card draw.
  • Main strengths: Access to powerful pieces that dodge the ban hammer (Zulaport Cutthroat, Phyrexian Altar, Dictate of Erebos, Blood Artist), skip combat by draining at range, and thrive on the graveyard.
  • Key weakness: Needs several key pieces — sacrifice fodder, an engine, and a wincon — so a bad opening hand can derail the game.
  • Top general: The Serpent Society feeds the sacrifice loop itself, forcing each opponent to sacrifice a creature on every deathtouch death.
The Serpent Society3,880 decks
A sacrifice-burn engine where deathtouch deaths drain the whole table.
General adds: The Serpent Society has deathtouch and, whenever another deathtouch creature you control dies, forces each opponent to sacrifice a nontoken creature.

🔮 Spellslinger — Video #10
  • Strategy core: Chain a massive number of instants and sorceries, mostly in Izzet (Blue-Red).
  • Main strengths: Threats stay hidden in hand so opponents underestimate you, then you win in a single turn with explosive spells like Mizzix's Mastery or Crackle with Power.
  • Key weakness: It's a one-time surprise: brutally effective in the first game, but opponents learn fast and start targeting you down.
  • Top general: Namor the Sub-Mariner is the archetype's top Marvel general, turning every blue noncreature spell into a Merfolk token army.
Namor the Sub-Mariner3,745 decks
A spellslinging Merfolk engine that spawns Merfolk equal to the blue pips in each noncreature spell.
General adds: Namor the Sub-Mariner creates 1/1 blue Merfolk tokens equal to the number of blue mana symbols in each noncreature spell you cast, and his power matches your Merfolk count.
