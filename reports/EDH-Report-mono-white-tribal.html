Myrel, Shield of Argive
  • Soldiers per swing — Each attack turns your entire board into fuel: Myrel spawns a 1/1 Soldier token for every Soldier you already control, so the army doubles with Lathril-like snowballing speed.
  • Opponent lockdown — During your turn opponents can't cast spells or activate abilities of artifacts, creatures, or enchantments, which is a stax-style tempo advantage that protects your combat step.
  • Mono-white banding identity — Pure white leans into small-bodied Soldiers, anthems, and token synergies, the classic 'go wide and pump' archetype with no color bleed.
  • The numbers — At 8,660 decks this is the most-played mono-white commander of the lot, and its signature Soldiers plan (1,070 decks) is far ahead of second-place Tokens (784).

Darien, King of Kjeldor
  • Soldiers from pain — Whenever you're dealt damage you create that many 1/1 white Soldier tokens, turning every hit against you into a growing army.
  • Lifegain pairing — Because the tokens replace your lost life with bodies that can block and later attack, Darien rewards pairing damage with lifelink and life-gain effects.
  • Mono-white resilience — White offers the defensive tools (protection, board wipes, lifegain) that let you survive the damage you're converting into Soldiers.
  • The numbers — With 4,732 decks the king sits near the top; its signature Tokens plan (385) and Lifegain (384) are nearly tied as the defining pairs.

Preston, the Vanisher
  • Copy-on-entry — Whenever another nontoken creature enters without being cast, Preston creates a 0/1 white Illusion token copy of it, doubling all your blink and reanimation payoffs.
  • Blink engine — Because copies trigger on un-cast entries, blink (exile-and-return) effects chain infinite value as each enter triggers a fresh copy.
  • Built-in removal — Sacrificing five Illusions lets you exile any nonland permanent, a board-control valve for surplus copies.
  • The numbers — At 3,052 decks Preston is a combo favorite; his signature Blink plan (499) dwarfs Tokens (177) and Combo (88).
