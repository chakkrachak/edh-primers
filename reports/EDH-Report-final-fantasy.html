20 commanders from the Final Fantasy Universes Beyond set, ranked by EDHREC popularity and grouped by color identity. Each row: left = the hero (card art + character + why), right = the top-6 EDHREC game plans with the character’s contribution. Sources: EDHREC (popularity, tags), Scryfall (art, Oracle).
