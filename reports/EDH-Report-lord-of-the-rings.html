🧝 Lord of the Rings Commanders — EDH Report

20 heroes and villains from the Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth set, ranked by EDHREC popularity and grouped by color identity. Each row: left = the character (card art + who they are + why), right = the top-6 EDHREC game plans with their contribution. Sources: EDHREC (popularity, tags), Scryfall (art, Oracle).

⚪ Mono-White
Gandalf the White
  • Flash everything — Gandalf lets you cast legendary spells and artifact spells as though they had flash, so your threats arrive at instant speed.
  • Double the triggers — Whenever a legendary or artifact permanent enters or leaves the battlefield, the triggered abilities of your permanents fire an additional time.
  • Mono-white tricks — White brings flicker, recursion, and artifact-synergy pieces that exploit the doubling — a rare depth for the color.
  • Wizard of the West — At 2,932 decks, his signature theme is Artifacts (412 decks).

The Gaffer
  • Life points become cards: if you gained 3 or more life in a turn, you draw a card at each end step — including your opponents', doubling your draw every round.
  • Monowhite lifegain core: Soul Sisters, life-linkers, and token healers make the 3-life threshold trivial to hit every turn.
  • Simple, sturdy enabler: a cheap 3-drop that needs no activation, no attack, no setup beyond lifegain.
  • 721 decks with Lifegain as the signature plan (193 decks): tokens, counter, and stax variants all orbit the same draw engine.
