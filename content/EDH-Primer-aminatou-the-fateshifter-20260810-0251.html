1. Commander Sheet

Aminatou, the Fateshifter

Quick read

  • Flicker engine : her −1 exiles a permanent you own and returns it immediately — every ETB trigger cascades.
  • Topdeck control : her +1 controls the top of your library (miracle synergy), and the ultimate literally steals the win.
  • Recursion loop : flicker Soulherder / Felidar Guardian / Peregrine Drake for infinite mana and ETBs.

2. Chosen Game Plan: 🌀 BLINK / FLICKER — The signature plan (771 decks)

🌀 BLINK / FLICKER CHOSEN PLAN

The dominant plan for Aminatou (771 decks on EDHREC).

How it plays
  • Blink engine : her −1 exiles a permanent you own and returns it immediately — ETB effects trigger on loop.
  • Value every turn : re-play Mulldrifter, Baleful Strix and friends to draw cards and control the board.
Core loop
  • Blinkers : Felidar Guardian and Displacer Kitten blink other permanents in turn, creating infinite chains.
  • Amplifiers : Ephemerate (rebound) and Soulherder (passive blink) multiply; Panharmonicon doubles every ETB.
Win conditions
  • Card advantage : every blink = draw/removal — the deck out-values the table.
  • Infinite mana : Peregrine Drake / Archaeomancer blinking each other → X-spell, extra turns or mill (Altar of the Brood).
📰 Secondary source: "Archetypes of Imagination - Making a Flicker Deck" (EDHREC Articles): the guide breaks a flicker deck into short categories of ETB creatures — the land fetchers (Loyal Warhound, Knight of the White Orchid to keep up with ramp), the removal creatures (Loran of the Third Path, Fiend Hunter, Riptide Gearhulk, interacting with every opponent on every entry) and the finishers (Moonshaker Cavalry, a Craterhoof Behemoth that repeats once blinked). Our list follows that skeleton: ramp fetchers (Solemn Simulacrum), ETB removal (Baleful Strix, Venser) and blink finishers (Peregrine Drake as infinite mana).
