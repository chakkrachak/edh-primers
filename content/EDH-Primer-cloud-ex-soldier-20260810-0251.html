1. Commander Sheet

Cloud, Ex-SOLDIER

Quick read

  • Free equip : it equips for free on entry — every Equipment in hand hits the board immediately.
  • Draw engine : draws massively on attack (1 card per equipped creature).
  • Treasures : past 7 power every attack generates Treasures — ramp from combat damage.
  • Naya package : access to the best Equipment support (Puresteel Paladin, Tifa, Colossus Hammer).

2. Chosen Game Plan: ⚔️ VOLTRON EQUIPMENT — The signature plan (2,760 Equipment decks)

⚔️ VOLTRON EQUIPMENT CHOSEN PLAN

The dominant plan for Cloud (2,760 Equipment decks + 1,568 Voltron on EDHREC).

How it plays
  • Free equip : Cloud equips for free on entry — every Equipment in hand hits the board immediately.
  • Draw per attacker : each attack draws a card per equipped attacking creature.
  • War machine : Colossus Hammer (+10/+10), Champion's Helm (+2/+2 + hexproof) push Cloud past 7 power → Treasures.
Core loop
  • Draw engine : Puresteel Paladin (free equip with Metalcraft), Sigarda's Aid / Forge Anew (free flash equips).
  • Doubling : Cloud, Midgar Mercenary doubles the triggers.
Win conditions
  • One-shot : commander damage from Colossus Hammer.
  • Infinite combats : Aggravated Assault + Sword of Feast and Famine, Aurelia + Helm of the Host.
  • Extra phases : Tifa / Aurelia combat phases.
📰 Secondary source: “EDHREC Guide to Equipment in Commander” (Cooper Gottfried, edhrec.com): the official EDHREC guide identifies 4 pillars of an Equipment deck: ① the equipment themselves (cheap-to-equip weapons — Mask of Memory, Lavaspur Boots — or powerful finishers), ② the free-equip engines (Puresteel Paladin, Sigarda’s Aid, Brass Squire, Forge Anew), ③ card draw tied to attacking with equipped creatures, and ④ protection (hexproof, indestructible, boots). The guide’s core insight: an Equipment deck lives and dies by its ability to re-equip fast after a wipe — Cloud’s free self-equip on entry plus Puresteel Paladin make it one of the best shells for that exact engine.
