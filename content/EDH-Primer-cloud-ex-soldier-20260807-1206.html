Exact Oracle text

Haste

When Cloud enters, attach up to one target Equipment you control to it.

Whenever Cloud attacks, draw a card for each equipped attacking creature you control. Then if Cloud has power 7 or greater, create two Treasure tokens.

Quick read

The ultimate equipment-voltron general: it equips for free on entry, draws massively on attack (1 card per equipped creature), and generates Treasures past 7 power. Naya gives access to the best Equipment tutors and free-equip engines (Puresteel Paladin, Sigarda's Aid, Forge Anew) — turn Cloud into a 14/14 hasty monster and win through commander damage or infinite combats.

2. Chosen Game Plan: ⚔️ VOLTRON EQUIPMENT — The signature plan (2,760 Equipment decks)

⚔️ VOLTRON EQUIPMENT CHOSEN PLAN

The dominant plan for Cloud (2,760 Equipment decks + 1,568 Voltron on EDHREC).

  • How it plays: Cloud equips for free on entry, and each attack draws a card per equipped attacking creature. Equip it with Colossus Hammer (+10/+10), Champion's Helm (+2/+2 + hexproof) and friends to turn it into a 7+ power war machine that generates Treasures.
  • Core engine: the Equipment draw engine — Puresteel Paladin (free equip with Metalcraft), Sigarda's Aid / Forge Anew (free flash equips), and Cloud, Midgar Mercenary doubling the triggers.
  • Win conditions: one-shot commander damage from Colossus Hammer, infinite combats (Aggravated Assault + Sword of Feast and Famine, Aurelia + Helm of the Host), or Tifa/Aurelia extra combat phases.
📰 Secondary source: “EDHREC Guide to Equipment in Commander” (Cooper Gottfried, edhrec.com): the official EDHREC guide identifies 4 pillars of an Equipment deck: ① the equipment themselves (cheap-to-equip weapons — Mask of Memory, Lavaspur Boots — or powerful finishers), ② the free-equip engines (Puresteel Paladin, Sigarda’s Aid, Brass Squire, Forge Anew), ③ card draw tied to attacking with equipped creatures, and ④ protection (hexproof, indestructible, boots). The guide’s core insight: an Equipment deck lives and dies by its ability to re-equip fast after a wipe — Cloud’s free self-equip on entry plus Puresteel Paladin make it one of the best shells for that exact engine.
