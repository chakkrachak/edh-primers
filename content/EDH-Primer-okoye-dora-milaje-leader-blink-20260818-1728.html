2. Chosen Game Plan: BLINK — The chosen plan (10 decks)

🌀 Blink CHOSEN PLAN

The Blink plan for Okoye, Dora Milaje Leader (10 decks on EDHREC), vs Tokens (10).

How it plays
  • Core : Okoye creates two Soldiers every time she enters, so the plan is to flicker her repeatedly to re-make tokens and reset her value.
  • Engine : Repeatable blinks like Teleportation Circle, Conjurer's Closet and Eldrazi Displacer re-trigger Okoye (and other enters-the-battlefield creatures) every turn.
  • Payoff : Instant flickers — Cloudshift, Ephemerate, Eerie Interlude — also dodge removal and save your board, turning blink into both value and protection.
Win conditions
  • Re-entry value : Each blink re-creates two Soldiers and re-triggers other enters-the-battlefield abilities for a growing army.
  • Protect the board : Instant-speed mass blinks like Eerie Interlude and Lae'zel's Acrobatics save your creatures from wipes and removal.
  • Value blink : Flickerwisp, Restoration Angel and Glorious Protector re-trigger your best ETB creatures for extra tokens and board impact.
