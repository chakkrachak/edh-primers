1. Commander Sheet

Shu Yun, the Silent Tempest

Quick read

  • Prowess pump : every noncreature spell pumps him (+1/+1).
  • Double strike : {R/W}{R/W} grants double strike to any creature — usually himself.
  • Cantrip shell : 1-mana unblockable cantrips (Slip Through Space, Distortion Strike) chain into a one-shot machine.

2. Chosen Game Plan: ⚔️ VOLTRON — The signature plan (96 decks)

⚔️ VOLTRON CHOSEN PLAN

The dominant plan for Shu Yun (96 Voltron decks, 94 Spellslinger, 62 Prowess on EDHREC).

How it plays
  • Open mana : cast Shu Yun on turn 3, then keep {R/W}{R/W} up every turn.
  • One spell = threat : any noncreature spell (cantrip, pump, protection) makes him unblockable (Slip Through Space, Distortion Strike, Aqueous Form) and grants double strike.
  • Prowess : each spell also pumps him.
Core loop
  • Cantrip chain : cantrip → draw + prowess + unblockable → swing with double strike → commander damage.
  • Rebound : Distortion Strike and Emerge Unscathed cast twice; Feather, the Redeemed returns target spells to hand each turn.
  • Draw : Whirlwind of Thought turns every spell into a card.
Win conditions
  • 21 damage : two swings with double strike + Unleash Fury / Brute Force / Legion Leadership.
  • Multipliers : Inquisitor's Flail and Runechanter's Pike; Jeska, Thrice Reborn makes it triple.
  • Tutor : Sunforger fetches the finisher or the protection.
📰 Secondary source: “Building Monk Typal With Shu Yun, the Silent Tempest” (EDHREC Articles — deck tech on Shu Yun): the guide calls Shu Yun “maybe not the strongest Monk commander, but… the most aggressive” and stresses that the ability to “give out double strike to any creature” is what makes him fun and versatile. Its core advice maps 1:1 onto our Voltron plan: play cheap cantripping noncreature spells (“We want spells that cantrip, like Opt”) so every spell triggers prowess and leaves mana up to activate double strike, and use evasive creatures that “make solid use of our commander’s ability to grant double strike.”
