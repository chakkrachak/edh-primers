1. Commander Sheet

Terra, Herald of Hope

Quick read

  • Core mechanic : Terra fills her own graveyard (Trance: mill 2 + flying at combat) then reanimates threats for free.
  • Engine : Celes, Stitcher's Supplier and Priest of Fell Rites mill; Reanimate and Karmic Guide bring back the board.
  • Payoff : free reanimation of big threats every turn.
  • Reanimate : a threat reanimated each turn.
