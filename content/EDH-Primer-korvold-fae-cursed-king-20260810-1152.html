1. Commander Sheet

Korvold, Fae-Cursed King

Quick read

  • Sacrifice value engine : every permanent you sacrifice draws a card and grows Korvold — the whole deck loops sac outlets, fodder and payoffs.
  • Two dominant plans : Sacrifice (1,626 decks) and Treasure (1,589) — the treasure generators ARE the best sac fodder, so the plans merge naturally.
  • Combo-ready : Pitiless Plunderer + Ashnod's Altar + any recursive creature = infinite mana, and Korvold's draw finds the pieces.

2. Chosen Game Plan: 🔥 SACRIFICE — The signature plan (1,626 decks)

🎯 Sacrifice CHOSEN PLAN

The Sacrifice plan for Korvold, Fae-Cursed King (1,626 decks on EDHREC), vs Treasure (1,589), Aristocrats (868).

How it plays
  • Core mechanic : Korvold turns every permanent you sacrifice into a +1/+1 counter AND a card — the classic Jund sac engine (Mayhem Devil pings, Viscera Seer/Ashnod's Altar fuel the loop).
  • Engine : sacrifice outlets + token/fetch fodder + draw-on-sac triggers (Korvold himself, Grim Haruspex-type effects).
  • Payoff : a giant flying Korvold that draws into more sac fodder every turn.
Win conditions
  • Draw into value : every sacrifice replaces itself — keep the engine fed and never run out of gas.
  • Commander damage : a 10/10+ flying Korvold closes in 2 hits.
  • Mayhem Devil drain : each sacrifice pings the table for 1-2, finishing low life totals.
