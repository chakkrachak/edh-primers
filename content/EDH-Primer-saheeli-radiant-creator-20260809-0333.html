⚡ ENERGY CHOSEN PLAN

The dominant plan for Saheeli, Radiant Creator (1,209 Energy decks, 745 Artifacts, 288 Tokens on EDHREC).

How it plays
  • Energy on every spell : each Artificer or artifact spell gives an energy counter.
  • Passive stockpile : energy accumulates, then pays for massive outlets (Aetherworks Marvel, Roil Cartographer, Architect of the Untamed).
  • Self-fueling : the artifacts you cast also provide the 3 for Saheeli's hasty 5/5 copy each combat.
Core loop
  • Energy economy : Decoction Module, Gonti's Aether Heart and Era of Innovation turn every permanent entering into .
  • Explosion : Izzet Generatorium (+1 per gain) and Aether Refinery (doubling) multiply production.
  • Payoff : Nissa, Worldsoul Speaker turns 8 into free permanent spells.
Win conditions
  • Infinite combats : Lightning Runner (8 = extra combat) copied or backed by Aetherwind Basker.
  • Infinite turns : Gonti's Aether Heart recurred by Academy Ruins and copied as a 5/5.
  • Finisher : Aetherflux Conduit (50 = 7 cards + free spells).
