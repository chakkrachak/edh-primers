1. Commander Sheet

Isshin, Two Heavens as One

Quick read

  • Double triggers : every "whenever X attacks" trigger on your permanents fires twice.
  • Go wide : token-makers (Adeline, Krenko, Anim Pakal) explode the board each combat.
  • Value attacks : attack triggers draw (Chivalric Alliance), ramp (Sword of the Animist, Curse of Opulence) and drain (Commissar Severina Raine).
  • Finish : Fervent Charge pumps or infinite-combat combos (Hellkite Charger + Fire Nation Palace).

2. Chosen Game Plan: TOKENS — The chosen plan (1,320 decks)

👥 Tokens CHOSEN PLAN

The Tokens plan for Isshin, Two Heavens as One (1,320 decks on EDHREC), vs Aggro (1,389), Attack Triggers (1,208).

How it plays
  • Core mechanic : every token-maker attack trigger fires twice — two tokens per opponent per combat.
  • Engine : Adeline, Krenko and Anim Pakal explode the board; Anointed Procession doubles the output.
  • Payoff : the token army fuels Craterhoof-style pumps, drains and convoke/convoke-adjacent effects.
Win conditions
  • Alpha strike : a critical mass of tokens boosted by Fervent Charge / Akroma's Will.
  • Drain : Brutal Hordechief and Commissar Severina Raine scale with the board.
  • Infinite combats : the token horde through Hellkite Charger + Fire Nation Palace.

📰 Secondary source: “Breaking Down Tradition: Isshin, Two Heavens as One” (Evan Moss, EDHREC Articles — guest deck tech on Isshin): the guide frames Isshin as “a value engine that wants you to attack” and notes that “Isshin decks tend to run pretty similarly — attack, create tokens, ramp, attack some more.” His key insight for our plan: keep the aggressive core but stack repeatable attack triggers (card advantage on attack, Treasure ramp on attack, protection to keep the attackers alive) so every combat step generates value. The guide’s emphasis on protecting your attacking engine maps onto our protection suite (Flawless Maneuver, Akroma’s Will) and its ramp advice onto the attack-ramp package (Sword of the Animist, Captain Lannery Storm).
