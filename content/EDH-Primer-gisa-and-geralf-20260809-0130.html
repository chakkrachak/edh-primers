Exact Oracle text

When Gisa and Geralf enters, mill four cards.

Once during each of your turns, you may cast a Zombie creature spell from your graveyard.

Quick read

The Zombie tribal / reanimator commander: it mills four cards on entry and lets you cast a Zombie from the graveyard each turn. The horde never dies — every sacrifice or board wipe is temporary. The lords (Diregraf Captain, Lord of the Accursed), the Rooftop Storm engine and the sacrifice altars turn the graveyard into a war machine: an overrunning army, a looped Gray Merchant drain, infinite combos.

2. Chosen Game Plan: 🧟 ZOMBIE TRIBAL — The signature plan (1,179 decks)

🧟 ZOMBIE TRIBAL CHOSEN PLAN

The dominant plan for Gisa and Geralf (1,179 Zombie decks, 488 Reanimator, 257 Graveyard on EDHREC).

  • How it plays: an army of Zombies that never dies. Gisa and Geralf mills four cards on entry and lets you re-cast a Zombie from the graveyard every turn — every loss is temporary. Lords grow the horde, and your Zombies' deaths become a resource (drain, draw, tokens).
  • Core loop: graveyard → battlefield — Gisa and Geralf, Havengul Lich and Liliana cast Zombies from the graveyard; Rooftop Storm makes them free; Necroduality doubles every Zombie that enters; the altars (Ashnod's, Phyrexian) turn sacrifices into mana.
  • Win conditions: the lord-buffed horde (Diregraf Captain, Death Baron, Liliana's Mastery) overrunning in combat, the repeated drain of Gray Merchant of Asphodel reanimated in a loop, or the infinite sacrifice combos (Gravecrawler + altar, Mikaeus + undying).
📰 Secondary source: “EDHREC Guide to Zombies in Commander” (Sikora, EDHREC Articles — the reference guide for the type): “Zombies make more Zombies. Then some more Zombies. And then a few more Zombies for good measure. Then they swing and win in a turn or two.” The guide confirms the type’s strength: Zombies are “nigh impossible to keep clear off the board” thanks to their recursion — “any of the important Zombies will just come back later.” Applied to Gisa and Geralf: every lord, every engine (Rooftop Storm, Necroduality, Diregraf Captain) is a must-kill, but everything comes back from the graveyard — that is the deck’s thesis.
