2. Chosen Game Plan: 💀 REANIMATOR — The signature plan (1,981 decks)

💀 REANIMATOR (aggro reanimation) CHOSEN PLAN

The dominant plan for Terra (1,981 decks on EDHREC).

  • How it plays: Terra fuels herself through Trance (mill two at each combat start) and then, on each hit, brings back a creature with power 3 or less from the graveyard for . You fill your graveyard with threats and replay them for free in combat.
  • Core mechanic: targeted mill + cheap reanimation — Reanimate, Animate Dead and Priest of Fell Rites return your big creatures for - mana, while Terra provides the constant flow of cards to the graveyard AND free reanimation on contact.
  • Win conditions: overrun with reanimated creatures plus Terra hitting with flying. Finishers: Rise of the Dark Realms (all creatures from ALL graveyards) or a massive Living Death.
📰 Secondary source: "EDHREC Guide to Reanimator in Commander" (EDHREC Articles): the guide explains that reanimator relies on direct tutoring to the graveyard (Buried Alive, Entomb — "you can put any bomb directly into your graveyard"), high-mana-value bombs played without paying (Sheoldred, Archon of Cruelty) and the deck's flexibility (from casual to competitive, with bridges toward sacrifice decks via sacrifice outlets). Our list follows that skeleton: Buried Alive/Entomb to fill, Reanimate/Animate Dead to cheat, and a sacrifice axis (Ashnod's Altar, Goblin Bombardment).
