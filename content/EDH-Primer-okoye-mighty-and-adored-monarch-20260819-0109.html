Quick read

  • Become king : Okoye makes you the monarch the moment she enters, so you draw an extra card every turn while you keep the crown.
  • Pump and slam : At the beginning of combat she puts a +1/+1 counter on a creature that gains double strike and trample while attacking the monarch — a growing one-shot attacker.

2. Chosen Game Plan: MONARCH — The chosen plan (194 decks)

🎯 Monarch CHOSEN PLAN

The Monarch plan for Okoye, Mighty and Adored (194 decks on EDHREC).

How it plays
  • Core : Okoye becomes the monarch the moment she enters, so the whole plan is to claim the crown and keep it — every turn you hold it you draw an extra card and can pump a creature.
  • Engine : At the beginning of combat she puts a +1/+1 counter on target creature and gives it double strike and trample while it attacks the monarch — magnified by Doubling Season, Branching Evolution and Hardened Scales into a one-shot attacker.
  • Payoff : Court pieces (Court of Grace, Palace Jailer, Regal Behemoth, Throne of the High City) keep the crown and flood tokens, then a buffed double-striking attacker or Akroma's Will closes the game.
Win conditions
  • Buffed double striker : Okoye's combat counter — doubled by Doubling Season and Branching Evolution — turns a designated creature into a lethal double-striking, trampling attacker against the monarch.
  • Angel swarm : While you hold the crown, Court of Grace makes a 4/4 flying Angel each upkeep, and Anointed Procession or Doubling Season doubles them into a flying army.
  • Akroma's Will : With your commander on the field you choose both modes, giving the whole team double strike, flying, vigilance, lifelink and protection — one attack wipes the table.
