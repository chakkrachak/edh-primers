1. Commander Sheet

Marwyn, the Nurturer

Quick read

  • Core : Marwyn grows a +1/+1 counter for every other Elf that enters, so the plan is to flood the board with cheap Elves that double as mana and bodies.
  • Engine : Lords like Elvish Archdruid and Leaf-Crowned Visionary pump the whole team while Priest of Titania and Marwyn convert Elf count into huge mana.
  • Payoff : Spend the flood of mana on Ezuri-style overruns or a massive Marwyn tap to close out the game through combat.
  • Ezuri overrun : Activate Ezuri, Renegade Leader to give every Elf +3/+3 and trample, turning a wide board into a one-shot kill.

🎯 +1/+1 Counters — 320 decks

📖 Game plan

  • Core : Marwyn's own counter engine is the plan's backbone — every Elf that enters stacks a +1/+1 counter on her.
  • Engine : Immaculate Magistrate dumps counters equal to your Elf count while token makers keep triggering Marwyn's growth ability.
  • Payoff : A counter-fat Marwyn taps for enormous mana or swings for lethal commander damage.

🏆 Win conditions

  • Giant Marwyn : A heavily-countered Marwyn taps for a mountain of green mana or attacks for lethal commander damage.
  • Counter snowball : Doublers like Gilder Bairn and pump effects turn each counter into exponential board growth.
  • Powered pump : Raggadragga and counter-based payoffs convert accumulated counters into a game-ending attack.

🎯 Combo — 253 decks

📖 Game plan

  • Core : Marwyn's tap ability scales with her power, so the plan is to untap her repeatedly for infinite or near-infinite mana.
  • Engine : Untap outlets — Staff of Domination, Umbral Mantle, Quirion Ranger and Wirewood Symbiote — recycle her taps.
  • Payoff : Turn infinite mana into draws, damage or endless creatures to win on the spot.

🏆 Win conditions

  • Staff loop : With a 5+ power Marwyn, Staff of Domination untaps her for {3} and draws a card for {5}, chaining into your whole deck.
  • Umbral Mantle : Equip for free and pay {3} to untap Marwyn with +2/+2, netting mana every loop for infinite green.
  • Untap loops : Ashaya plus Scryb Ranger, or Temur Sabertooth plus untappers and Witness recursion, generate infinite mana into a finisher.

🎯 Ramp — 222 decks

📖 Game plan

  • Core : Marwyn is a mana dork whose output equals her power, so the plan maximizes mana per Elf and per land.
  • Engine : Priest of Titania, Elvish Archdruid, Quirion Ranger and Wirewood Lodge multiply your mana several times over.
  • Payoff : Race to enormous mana and cast game-ending spells far ahead of the curve.

🏆 Win conditions

  • Big-mana spells : Cast huge payoffs like Wurmcalling, Akroma's Memorial or a massive Fanning the Flames earlier than anyone else.
  • Super-tap : Untapping a fat Marwyn multiple times in one turn produces dozens of green mana.
  • Dork swarm : With several Elf dorks each tapping for multiple mana, no other deck can match your ramp.
