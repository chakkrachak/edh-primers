The Miracle Worker list is built AROUND Aminatou's enchantment-miracles ability: 15 enchantments + 8 enchantress-style payoffs (Mesa Enchantress, Ondu Spiritdancer, Sigil of the Empty Throne, One with the Multiverse, Sphere of Safety...). 10 of the deck's cards sit in the EDHREC High Synergy pool of BOTH the Enchantress (2,872 decks) and Topdeck (2,108) plans — the two dominant archetypes. Control (282) and Pillow Fort (154) are supported by the same cards (Sphere of Safety, Shark Typhoon, removal suite). Tokens/Combo are incidental: Sigil of the Empty Throne and Extravagant Replication make tokens but the list has no token payoffs beyond them. Verdict: the list is a classic Enchantress/Miracles control deck — Enchantress and Topdeck are co-favored, with Pillow Fort as the natural secondary plan.
