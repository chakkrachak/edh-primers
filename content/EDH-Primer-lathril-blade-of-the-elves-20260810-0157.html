1. Commander Sheet

Lathril, Blade of the Elves

Quick read

  • Token army : creates a token with every combat hit — wide board in a few swings.
  • Drain gun : her activation drains 10 life per opponent from the token army.
  • Elfball synergy : Elvish Archdruid / Priest of Titania / Imperious Perfect multiply mana and tokens.

2. Chosen Game Plan: 🧝 ELFBALL — Elf Tribal (5,242 decks)

🧝 ELFBALL CHOSEN PLAN

The dominant plan for Lathril (5,242 Elf decks on EDHREC).

How it plays
  • Cheap elves : a maximum of 1-3 mana Elves that boost each other.
  • Free tokens : Lathril is your token generator — every point of combat damage creates that many 1/1 Elf Warriors.
  • Lords scale : the more Elves you have, the bigger your lords get.
Core loop
  • Elf ramp : Priest of Titania, Elvish Archdruid produce mana → chain 5-6 Elves per turn.
  • Drain : activate Lathril with ten Elves to drain 10 life from each opponent (, tap ten untapped Elves you control).
Win conditions
  • Mass drain : repeated Lathril activations.
  • Overrun : Craterhoof Behemoth, Ezuri, Renegade Leader.
  • Infinite mana : Staff of Domination, Umbral Mantle.
📰 Secondary source: “Building Lathril, Blade of the Elves on a $40 Budget” (EDHREC Articles — the budget reference guide): the guide stresses the importance of evasion for Lathril (Cultist of the Absolute, Demonic Embrace, Zephyr Boots, Rope + Vorrac Battlehorns to make her unblockable), card advantage (Realmwalker, Miara, Champions of the Perfect, Pact of the Serpent as massive draw) and value engines to roll out the elf army before striking.
