Exact Oracle text

Trample

Whenever a land card is put into your graveyard from anywhere, put a +1/+1 counter on Slogurk.

Remove three +1/+1 counters from Slogurk: Return it to its owner's hand.

When Slogurk leaves the battlefield, return up to three target land cards from your graveyard to your hand.

Quick read

  • Lands matter : every land that dies or is milled makes him bigger (+1/+1).
  • Loop : sacrifice and replay your lands (Loam, fetchlands, Zuran Orb) — Slogurk becomes enormous.
  • Value engine : Tatyova / Azusa / Titania draw and ramp off every land replay.

2. Chosen Game Plan: 🌍 LANDS MATTER / LOAM — The signature plan (337 decks)

🌍 LANDS MATTER / LOAM CHOSEN PLAN

The dominant plan for Slogurk (337 Lands Matter decks + 31 Landfall on EDHREC).

How it plays
  • Graveyard lands : play lands from your graveyard in a loop — Life from the Loam (dredge 3) brings back 3 lands per turn.
  • Replay : Ramunap Excavator replays them; every land that dies makes Slogurk grow (+1/+1).
  • Sacrifice triggers : Zuran Orb and fetchlands sacrifice lands to trigger Slogurk again and again.
Core loop
  • Loam engine : Life from the Loam + cycling lands (Tranquil Thicket, Lonely Sandbar) = draw + graveyard filling + land replay.
  • Mass recursion : Splendid Reclamation brings back your ENTIRE land graveyard at once.
Win conditions
  • Giant Slogurk : +1/+1 per dead land makes him enormous.
  • Token army : Titania, Protector of Argoth turns sacrificed lands into 5/3 tokens — lethal board.
📰 Secondary source: "Building Six: An Inside-Out Mono-Green Artifacts Deck" (EDHREC Articles, about Six, one of the deck\u2019s key cards): the guide explains the retrace / graveyard lands mechanic — \u201cSix allows you to cast stuff from your graveyard, at the puny cost of discarding a land\u201d — and warns that \u201cplaying less than 45 is going to hamper dramatically your chances to pull it off.\u201d It highlights the \u201cvery cheap outlets that can be recurred ad nauseam\u201d (Lotus Petal, baubles) and the land recursion that fills the graveyard before bringing everything back — exactly the engine of the Slogurk deck (Loam + fetchlands + Zuran Orb).
