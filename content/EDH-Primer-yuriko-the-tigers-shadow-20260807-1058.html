Exact Oracle text

Commander ninjutsu (, Return an unblocked attacker you control to hand: Put this card onto the battlefield from your hand or the command zone tapped and attacking.)

Whenever a Ninja you control deals combat damage to a player, reveal the top card of your library and put that card into your hand. Each opponent loses life equal to that card's mana value.

Quick read

The unique Dimir aggro/control commander: commander ninjutsu lets her return from the command zone at will for ~2 mana. Every Ninja hit = draw 1 + drain equal to the mana value of the revealed card — hence the deck plays high-mana-value cards (expensive spells cast almost for free via ninjutsu or topdeck): each hit can deal 10+ damage to the whole table.

2. Chosen Game Plan: 🗡️ NINJUTSU TRIBAL — The signature plan (5,377 decks)

🗡️ NINJUTSU TRIBAL CHOSEN PLAN

The dominant plan for Yuriko (5,377 decks on EDHREC).

  • How it plays: attack with unblockable 1/1s, then return them to hand via ninjutsu to put Ninjas in their place — including Yuriko herself, whose commander ninjutsu ( from the command zone) makes her cost ~2 mana every turn. Each Ninja that connects triggers Yuriko's reveal.
  • Core mechanic: the reveal — every hit from a Ninja reveals the top card of your library; high-mana-value cards (Temporal Trespass, Shadow of Mortality) turn into mass damage to the whole table, while topdeck control (Brainstorm, Sensei's Divining Top) sets them up.
  • Win conditions: the accumulated drain of Yuriko's reveals (10+ damage per hit to every opponent) + the card advantage of draw Ninjas (Ninja of the Deep Hours, Ingenious Infiltrator). Fallen Shinobi additionally steals opponents' spells.
📰 Secondary source: “The Top 10 Most Played Ninjutsu Cards in Commander” (EDHREC Articles — the reference guide for the mechanic): the guide confirms Ingenious Infiltrator is played in 86% of Yuriko decks (“the lord that turns the rest of your army into Ninja of the Deep Hours”) and stresses the importance of evasion (Slither Blade, Changeling Outcast to guarantee the hit) and ninjutsu tricks (Cover of Darkness, Smoke Shroud) so that every combat generates advantage.
