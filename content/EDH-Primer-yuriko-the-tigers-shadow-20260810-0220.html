1. Commander Sheet

Yuriko, the Tiger's Shadow

Quick read

  • Commander ninjutsu : returns from the command zone at will for ~2 mana.
  • Hit = value : every Ninja hit draws 1 and drains equal to the revealed card's mana value.
  • Topdeck bombs : high-mana-value cards (Temporal Trespass, Shadow of Mortality) deal 10+ to the whole table.

2. Chosen Game Plan: 🗡️ NINJUTSU TRIBAL — The signature plan (5,377 decks)

🗡️ NINJUTSU TRIBAL CHOSEN PLAN

The dominant plan for Yuriko (5,377 decks on EDHREC).

How it plays
  • Unblockable enablers : attack with 1/1s that can't be blocked.
  • Ninjutsu swap : return them to hand via ninjutsu to put Ninjas in their place — including Yuriko herself (commander ninjutsu from the command zone, ~2 mana every turn).
  • Reveal : each Ninja that connects triggers Yuriko's reveal.
Core loop
  • The reveal : every hit reveals the top card of your library; high-mana-value cards (Temporal Trespass, Shadow of Mortality) turn into mass damage to the whole table.
  • Topdeck control : Brainstorm and Sensei's Divining Top set up the bombs.
Win conditions
  • Accumulated drain : Yuriko's reveals deal 10+ damage per hit to every opponent.
  • Draw Ninjas : Ninja of the Deep Hours, Ingenious Infiltrator fuel the hand.
  • Theft : Fallen Shinobi steals opponents' spells.
📰 Secondary source: “The Top 10 Most Played Ninjutsu Cards in Commander” (EDHREC Articles — the reference guide for the mechanic): the guide confirms Ingenious Infiltrator is played in 86% of Yuriko decks (“the lord that turns the rest of your army into Ninja of the Deep Hours”) and stresses the importance of evasion (Slither Blade, Changeling Outcast to guarantee the hit) and ninjutsu tricks (Cover of Darkness, Smoke Shroud) so that every combat generates advantage.
