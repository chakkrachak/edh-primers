1. Commander Sheet

Aminatou, Veil Piercer

Quick read

  • Engine : play enchantments to draw cards — Mesa Enchantress, Ondu Spiritdancer (doubles auras), Starfield Mystic (discount).
  • Miracle synergy : Aminatou's miracle −{4} makes every enchantment in hand a cheap blowout; surveil 2 each upkeep feeds both the enchantment count and the miracle trigger.
  • Snowball : each enchantment cantrips, the board grows, and control pieces (Doomwake Giant constellation) keep the table in check.
  • Angel army : Sigil of the Empty Throne turns every enchantment into a 4/4 Angel.

👑 Enchantress — 2872 decks

📖 Game plan

  • Engine : play enchantments to draw cards — Mesa Enchantress, Ondu Spiritdancer (doubles auras), Starfield Mystic (discount).
  • Miracle synergy : Aminatou's miracle −{4} makes every enchantment in hand a cheap blowout; surveil 2 each upkeep feeds both the enchantment count and the miracle trigger.
  • Snowball : each enchantment cantrips, the board grows, and control pieces (Doomwake Giant constellation) keep the table in check.

🏆 Win conditions

  • Angel army : Sigil of the Empty Throne turns every enchantment into a 4/4 Angel.
  • Copy engine : Extravagant Replication doubles your best permanent each upkeep.
  • Top-of-library : One with the Multiverse plays from the top of your library.
  • Finisher : Redress Fate recursion or a giant Entreat the Angels (miracle for {W}{W}{W}{W}).

👑 Topdeck — 2108 decks

📖 Game plan

  • Setup : surveil 2 at upkeep + cantrips (Brainstorm, Ponder, Portent, Telling Time), Halimar Depths and Brainstone stack the top of the library.
  • Payoff : miracle draws always hit — Aminatou's discount turns any enchantment into a cheap answer.
  • Engine : One with the Multiverse turns the top of the library into an extra hand.

🏆 Win conditions

  • Free spells : Aminatou's Augury casts your whole top 8 for free.
  • Cheap wipe : Terminus becomes a one-mana wipe via miracle.
  • Beatdown : Entreat the Angels and Shark Typhoon finish from the top.
  • Infinite turns : Timestream Navigator + Extravagant Replication (see combos).

🎯 Control — 282 decks

📖 Game plan

  • Answers : Swords to Plowshares, Path to Exile, Utter End, Cast Out, Return to Dust, Arcane Denial + counterspells.
  • Refill : the enchantress engine draws cards back between answers.
  • Recurring wipe : Doomwake Giant's constellation clears small boards repeatedly.

🏆 Win conditions

  • Reset then win : Terminus / Time Wipe clear the board, then the enchantress card advantage takes over.
  • Beats : Angel tokens, Dream Eater, or a copied engine via Mirrormade / Extravagant Replication.
  • Infinite ETB : Sharuum the Hegemon + Mirrormade loops (see combos).
