1. Commander Sheet

Sisay, Weatherlight Captain

Quick read

  • Toolbox tutor : {W}{U}{B}{R}{G} searches for any legendary permanent with mana value less than her power.
  • Grows with legends : +1/+1 per color among your other legends — the tutor range widens as you build.
  • Instant answers : fetch the right legend for any situation (Derevi, Jhoira, The One Ring).

2. Chosen Game Plan: 🏛️ LEGENDS / TOOLBOX — The signature plan (1,419 decks)

🏛️ LEGENDS / TOOLBOX CHOSEN PLAN

The dominant plan for Sisay (1,419 Legends decks, 1,093 Historic, 837 cEDH on EDHREC).

How it plays
  • Ramp first : 5-color dorks (Faeburrow Elder, Bloom Tender, Birds of Paradise) get Sisay out fast.
  • Repeatable toolbox : her {W}{U}{B}{R}{G} ability searches any legendary permanent with mana value less than her power and puts it onto the battlefield.
  • Scaling : more colors among your legends = bigger Sisay = bigger tutors.
Core loop
  • Multiple activations : untap engines (Derevi on attack, Ioreth, Minamo, Great Hall of the Citadel) let Sisay tutor several times per turn.
  • Right legend for the moment : removal (Boseiju), draw (Esper Sentinel), protection (Teferi), finishers (Jodah, Nicol Bolas).
Win conditions
  • Legend army : pumped by Jodah / Heroes' Podium / Flowering of the White Tree.
  • Post-wipe : Primevals' Glorious Rebirth.
  • Infinite mana : tutorable combos (Derevi + Emiel blink, Kinnan + untap aura) into a huge finale.
📰 Secondary source: “Hidden Gems: Sisay, Weatherlight Captain” (EDHREC Articles — deck tech on Sisay): the guide highlights Sisay’s unique role as a repeatable legendary tutor and stresses that the deck wins by having “the right legendary for the right situation” — a toolbox philosophy. It recommends stacking 5-color mana producers so Sisay can activate early and often, and pairing her with untap effects to chain multiple searches per turn. That maps directly onto our build: dorks (Faeburrow Elder, Bloom Tender, Selvala) power the engine, untappers (Derevi, Ioreth, Minamo) double it, and the legend pool covers every axis — ramp, draw, removal, protection and finishers.
