Exact Oracle text

If a creature attacking causes a triggered ability of a permanent you control to trigger, that ability triggers an additional time.

Quick read

The attack-trigger doubler: every "whenever X attacks" trigger on your permanents fires twice. Go wide with token-makers (Adeline, Krenko, Anim Pakal), draw from attack triggers (Chivalric Alliance, Aurelia), ramp with Sword of the Animist and Curse of Opulence — then finish with Fervent Charge pumps, extra combats (Aurelia, Aggravated Assault) or the infinite-combat combos.

2. Chosen Game Plan: ⚔️ ATTACK TRIGGERS — The signature plan (1,204 decks)

⚔️ ATTACK TRIGGERS CHOSEN PLAN

The dominant plan for Isshin (1,204 Attack Triggers decks, 1,389 Aggro, 1,316 Tokens on EDHREC).

  • How it plays: Isshin doubles every attack-triggered ability — attack with token-makers (Adeline, Krenko, Anim Pakal, Caesar), each trigger fires twice and the board explodes. Attack triggers also draw (Chivalric Alliance, Aurelia, the Law Above), ramp (Sword of the Animist, Curse of Opulence, Diamond Pick-Axe) and drain (Commissar Severina Raine, Brutal Hordechief).
  • Core loop: attack → doubled triggers → more tokens/draw/ramp → attack again. Windcrag Siege in Mardu mode is a second Isshin (quadruple triggers on your best attackers); Aurelia, the Warleader adds extra combat phases so the loop runs twice per turn.
  • Win conditions: the go-wide board pumped by Fervent Charge / Akroma's Will; the drain of Brutal Hordechief and Commissar Severina Raine; or the infinite-combat combos (Hellkite Charger + Fire Nation Palace, Aggravated Assault lines).
📰 Secondary source: “Breaking Down Tradition: Isshin, Two Heavens as One” (Evan Moss, EDHREC Articles — guest deck tech on Isshin): the guide frames Isshin as “a value engine that wants you to attack” and notes that “Isshin decks tend to run pretty similarly — attack, create tokens, ramp, attack some more.” His key insight for our plan: keep the aggressive core but stack repeatable attack triggers (card advantage on attack, Treasure ramp on attack, protection to keep the attackers alive) so every combat step generates value. The guide’s emphasis on protecting your attacking engine maps onto our protection suite (Flawless Maneuver, Akroma’s Will) and its ramp advice onto the attack-ramp package (Sword of the Animist, Captain Lannery Storm).
